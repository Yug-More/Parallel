It is not evidence about the later implementation's quality, adoption or production readiness.

[Repository audit](REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md), [engineering decisions](ENGINEERING_DECISIONS.md) and dated execution/evaluation records preserve the implementation history. No history was rewritten and no collaborators were added.
